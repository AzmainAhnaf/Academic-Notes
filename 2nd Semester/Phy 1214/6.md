
Experiment Name: Determination of the Specific Rotation of Sugar Solution by Means of Polarimeter

**What are the required apparatus?**
Polarimeter, Balance, Measuring Cylinder, Sugar, Beaker, Thermometer, and a source of light.

**What is polarization?**
Light can be visualized as perpendicularly oscillating components of Electric Magnetic fields. The direction of oscillation of the electric field is the direction of polarization.

A normal light contains the electric components oriented in all directions but when it passed through a polarize then only the components in one direction are allowed to pass. This light is called as plane polarized light.


**What do you mean by optically active substances?**
Substances that rotate the plane of polarization are said to be optically active substances and the phenomenon is known as the optical activity or rotatory polarization.

**Mention some optically active substances**
These are sugar crystals, sugar solution, turpentine, sodium chlorate, tarter acids in aqueous solutions, solution of quinine sulfate, etc.

**Which substances are called dextro-rotatory?**
Those substances which rotate the plan of polarization in the clockwise direction (right side) when the observer faces the source of light. Also known as right handed substances.

**Which substances are called Laevo-rotatory?**
Those substances which rotate the plane of polarization in the anticlockwise direction (left side) when the observer faces the source of light. Also called left-handed substances.

**What do you understand from the specific rotation of sugar solution?**
When a plane polarized light passes through a tube containing optically active material. Then the plane of its polarization rotates.

The specific rotation is a measure of this rotation per unit concentration (g/cc) per unit length of the tube (in decimeter)
$$
\text{Specific Rotation} = \frac{\text{Rotation}(\theta)}{\text{Concentration} \times \text{Length of the tube}} 
$$


**Why a substance rotates the plane of polarized light**
To understand this concept first we should know that some molecules have mirror symmetry (achiral) while others don't (chiral)

In case of molecules with mirror symmetry - when light passes through its solution then there is a possibility that some molecules will rotate the angle of polarization towards one while its mirror imaged molecules will rotate it opposite. So overall the plane of polarization doesn't change.

In the case of molecules without mirror symmetry (chiral) - when light passes through its solution then one kind of its orientation will rotate it one side but as its mirror image is not possible the rotation cannot be cancelled. Sometimes it can even increase the angle of rotation. So there is an overall rotation of the polarization vector.

**What are those factors on which specific rotation depends?**
1. Thickness of the substance ($\theta \propto l$). Directly proportional to the length of the tube
2. Concentration of the solution ($\theta \propto c$). The angle is directly proportional to the concentration
3. Wavelength of light used ($\theta \propto \frac1{\lambda ^2}$). The angle is inversely proportional to square of the wavelength
4. This also depends upon the temperature on  the experiment is being conducted on.


**What is polarimeter and what are its parts?**
It is an instrument used to measure the rotation produced by a substance. When used for finding the optical rotation of sugar, it is also called saccharimeter


**Why sometimes it is also called Laurent's half shade polarimeter**
It is called such when a half shade plate is introduce in the system. It is place between the polarized and sample tube

It consists of two half-disks of equally absorptive material. One half is glass, and it lets the polarized light pass through unchanged. The other half is made of quartz, cut to a thickness of half-wave-plate.

**Why Lauren't half shade polarimeter is better than an ordinary polarimeter?**
Normally, when we try to find the angle of maximum light intensity using an analyzer then it is hard to spot the place where it is maximum because our eyes cannot judge the different in the brightness near the maxima.

Half shade plate helps to increase the accuracy of our measurement because it is easy for out eyes to judge one bright and one dark side on a single plate.

In this case we try to achieve the condition when both plates are equally bright. Other wise the condition on one brighter and than other can easily be analyzed by our eye
