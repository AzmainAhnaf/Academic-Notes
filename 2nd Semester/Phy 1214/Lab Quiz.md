
### Determination of the Value of Acceleration due to Gravity (g) by Means of a Compound Pendulum

For the experiment to work, when the amplitude is small enough to satisfy the condition $\sin \theta \approx \theta$. This is because a simple solution of the differential equation of compound pendulum only exist give the condition is fulfilled.

**Final Equation**:
$$
g = \frac{4 \pi ^2 (l_1 + l_2)}{T^2}
$$
Required apparatus for this experiment is
	A compound pendulum, stopwatch, meter scale, knife-edge, etc.

### Determination of the Frequency of a Tuning Fork by Melde's Apparatus

The stationary wave will form by superposition of the direct and reflected waves. Direct waves will form from the vibration of the tuning fork, upon reaching the other side of the scale, reflected wave will be constructed.

In transverse arrangement, the oscillation of tuning fork will be perpendicular with the length of the string.

In longitudinal arrangement, the oscillation of tuning fork will be parallel with the length of the string.

**Final Equation**
In transverse arrangement,
$$
N = n = \frac 1 \lambda \sqrt{\frac{T}{m}}
$$
In longitudinal arrangement,
$$
N = 2n = \frac 2 \lambda \sqrt{\frac Tm}
$$

### Determination of the Radius of Curvature of a Plano Convex Lens by Newtons Ring Method

**Final Equation**
$$
D_n = \sqrt{4n \lambda R}
$$
$$
D_{n + p} = \sqrt{4(n + p)\lambda R}
$$
$$
R = \frac{D_{n + p}^2 - D_n^2}{4p \lambda}
$$
